Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you order.
How do I order a service?

Message me on Telegram (@AGTREMOTECRAFT202) with the service you want and the details — video length, style, deadline. I'll confirm the exact price first, then share the payment number. Send the payment screenshot and work starts right away.

How long does it take?

Short videos (under 1 minute) usually take 1–2 days. Longer videos and bigger projects are agreed case by case — tell me your deadline and I'll do my best to match it.

How do I pay?

KBZPay or WavePay. For safety, the payment number is shared only after your order is confirmed — then just send the screenshot in our chat.

Can AI videos have a Burmese voiceover?

Yes — natural Burmese voiceover options are available for AI Video Creation, with or without a script written for you.

What should I prepare before ordering?

Your product photos or footage, any logo/text you want shown, and an example of a style you like. The clearer the brief, the faster and better the result.

Do you accept ads on your Telegram channel?

Not at the moment — the channel is knowledge-sharing only. What I do offer is making the ad video for your own business or page.

Do I need a computer for the AI Video Course?

No — the whole course is phone-only. Every lesson is done on a smartphone, from creating the AI images to the final CapCut export. If you have a phone and a Google account, you are ready.

How does the course work, and what if I fall behind?

Lessons are recorded videos posted in a private Telegram group, one week of modules at a time over 4 weeks. Learn at your own pace — the videos stay in the group after the course ends, and I answer questions daily during the 4 weeks.

How do I join Batch 1 of the course?

Message me on Telegram (@AGTREMOTECRAFT202). Batch 1 is 45,000 Ks, limited to 20 students. After I confirm your seat, you pay by KBZPay or WavePay, send the screenshot, and I add you to the private course group.
